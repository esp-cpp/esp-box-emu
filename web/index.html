This page needs WebUSB: open it in Chrome or Edge on a desktop (Safari and Firefox do not have it).

Plug in the emulator and connect

Connect over the USB-C port to see what the emulator is doing, browse and start games, watch and play the screen, move files to and from the SD card, change the settings and inspect the system.

Nothing to install: the page talks to the device's link interface (WebUSB). Close emulink first, only one program can use the link at a time.
